How a commission starts

First we understand what you have and what you need. Then we agree on what to build and what it costs, before we start.

  1. 01

    Assessment

    We review your case and send you back, free of charge, one page with what we would do, what we need from you and what is not worth doing.

  2. 02

    Fixed-price proposal

    Scope, price, timeline and how we will check that it works, in writing and before we start. You pay for the result, not for hours.

  3. 03

    First version

    A concrete piece with your real data, to review together and adjust.

  4. 04

    Delivery and use

    Running on your own devices, with instructions and checks. If you like, we can also look after its maintenance.
