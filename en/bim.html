Your next tender asks for BIM. Have you got it covered?

When the tender documents ask for it, this is how they ask: delivery in IFC, coordination under ISO 19650, a common data environment, security compliant with Spain's National Security Framework. And where they do not ask for it yet, the clock is ticking all the same.

What I do exactly

  • Information requirements. I read the tender documents and turn what they ask for into a BIM execution plan your team can follow without being experts.
  • Coordination and quality control. Model federation, clash detection, verification that what is delivered meets what was required, reports at each milestone.
  • Common data environment. Set up and kept in order, with version and access control, without forcing you to buy expensive platforms.
  • Deliverables. IFC, BCF, COBie and quantities that come out of the model, not out of a spreadsheet on the side.
  • Automation. Repetitive checks are done by a program, not by a person staring at screens. That is why the work comes back in days.
