What data is processed and what for

  • Contact emails. If you write to the contact address, the data you include is processed (your email address and the content of the message) for the sole purpose of answering your query and, if you ask for one, preparing a proposal. Legal basis: pre-contractual measures taken at your request and legitimate interest in attending to the communication (Article 6(1)(b) and 6(1)(f) GDPR). Messages are kept for as long as the conversation lasts and, if it leads to a professional relationship, for the duration of that relationship and the associated legal periods.
  • Audience measurement. This site uses GoatCounter, a visit counter that uses no cookies and does not identify the visitor; it only produces aggregate counts (page views, approximate origin). That is why this site shows no cookie banner: it does not use them.
  • Contact form. If you use the contact form, we process the information you provide: your name, organisation, email address, reason for contacting us and message. Your submission passes through FormSubmit before reaching ontos by email. This provider processes the content and, according to its documentation, keeps submissions in its archive for 30 days. ontos keeps the message while we are in contact and, if it leads to a professional relationship, for the duration of that relationship and any applicable legal retention periods. Legal basis: your consent when submitting the form (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can avoid this intermediary by writing directly to hola@ontosdigital.es.

What we do not do

ontos does not sell your data, build profiles or send advertising. We use your email solely to reply and deal with your request. The form uses the provider described above; its privacy policy says that its own service may use cookies. This site hosts its fonts itself, so it does not connect to third-party servers to load them.

Your rights

You can exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction and portability by writing to hola@ontosdigital.es. If you believe that any processing is not correct, you can lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
