Fernando Calle

Civil engineer · automation and AI · founder of ontos

I am a civil engineer, and my work is putting information in order with code and artificial intelligence. I come from the projects where you learn that the hard way: three years leading models, data and automation on major international works, where the order of the information sets the deadlines and the costs.

Today I work on two fronts that feed each other. I build ontos, the system that puts my own life in order, and I bring the same method to people and companies, from their context for AI to a website or a world in 3D.

Where I come from

I spent the three years before ontos on projects where the volume of information is beyond any one person, and the only way out is to build systems that put it in order on their own.

  • Sydney, Australia

    Sydney Metro West

    BIM Manager for the package of 41 cross passages. I automated their modelling and the loading of metadata to the client's requirements: what I first had to solve by hand ended up being solved by code.

  • Sydney, Australia

    Malabar

    BIM Coordinator at the Sydney Water treatment plant (Malabar Water Resource Recovery Facility).

  • Adelaide, Australia

    Northern Water

    BIM Manager for the desalination plant of the Northern Water Supply Project (140 ML/day). I managed the delivery of 67 models with a workflow I fully automated, supported by AI: the time saved on checks, exports and audits is worth the work of up to five people.

  • Saudi Arabia

    Red Sea Governorates Urban Development Plan

    Urban development plan for the Red Sea governorates, with GIS training for the team in Riyadh.

  • Tabuk, Saudi Arabia

    NEOM

    GIS and BIM execution plan for the tunnels of The Line.

Award and publication
