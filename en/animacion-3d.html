Little planet2D playground
The figure needs WebGL. You can visit the 2D playground.
The figure is resting.

Open the 2D playground
Drag to rotate · pinch or use the wheel to zoom

Look from the front, back, above and below. Dragging stops auto rotation. “Life” enables breathing and small gestures; turn it off to observe the figure still. “Figure only” fills the screen with ontos; return with the top button or Escape.

With the canvas focused: arrows to rotate, + and − to resize, Home to reset the view and H to wave. Centre also restores the standing pose.
