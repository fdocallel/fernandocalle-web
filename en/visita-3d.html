How to play · keyboard, camera and objective

WASD / ↑↓←→ move towards where you are looking · Space jump · Shift roll · E pick up, drop or plant · H wave · P pause. Drag the scenery to rotate the camera; use the mouse wheel to zoom. View planet shows the whole sphere; Follow ontos returns to the walk. On mobile, hold an arrow and use your other thumb to jump or drag the scenery. Pinch with two fingers to change distance.

Take the seed to the pedestal between the arches. The eight sparkles are an optional detour. You can go all the way around: gravity keeps you on the sphere. Restart begins a new garden.
